# Design notes (not published)

Subject: dated research notes from a one-person GPU lab, read by ML-systems and kernel
people, often arriving from a PR description or an HN thread. The page's job is to be a
citable record with the numbers and the figures in front of the reader; the index's job is
a gallery that still works with one entry.

Tokens
- paper `#F2F3F0` (matte, slightly cool), ink `#1A1D21`, muted `#5C636B`, rule `#CFD2CC`,
  accent `#6B2C91` (the violet of the magma colormap the figures use). Dark: `#15171B`,
  `#E4E6E2`, `#9AA1A9`, `#2C3137`, `#C58BE0`.
- Type: Fraunces (opsz axis, SOFT 50) for titles and the index statement; IBM Plex Sans for
  everything else, tabular numerals in tables; Plex Mono only inside code.
- Layout: left-aligned, 68ch prose column; figures and wide tables break out to the full
  content width (960px) on desktop; project pages carry a sticky contents list on the right
  from 1100px up. Index = statement, one featured note (figure left, text right), then a
  tile grid of earlier notes with no borders or shadows.

Deliberately avoided: cream + terracotta, black + acid green, hairline broadsheet, SaaS
cards, caps eyebrows, middle-dot meta strings, arrows on links, numbered markers.

The one bold element is the featured figure on the index: a dark expert-footprint map on
pale paper. Everything else stays quiet.
